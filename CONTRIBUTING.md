# Contributing to project-sol

Thanks for helping. The short version:

1. Fork, then branch from `staging`: `git switch -c feature/<name> staging`
2. Make your change and keep commits small (`feat:`, `fix:`, `docs:` prefixes).
3. Open a squash pull request into `staging` and explain what you changed and how you tested it.
4. Releases are cut from `staging` into `main` with a merge commit by the maintainer.
